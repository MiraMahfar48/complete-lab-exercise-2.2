I am currently enrolled as a first-year student at Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA), pursuing a Bachelor of Cyber Security with Honours.

Universiti Malaysia Pahang Al-Sultan Abdullah (UMPSA) is one of Malaysia's leading technical universities, renowned for its strong focus on engineering, technology, and practical education. With its state-of-the-art facilities and industry-aligned curriculum, the university provides a dynamic environment that empowers students to excel in the rapidly evolving digital world.

The university operates across two strategic campuses in Pahang, each with its own specialized focus. The Gambang Campus serves as the main hub for, technology, and foundation studies, located near the vibrant city of Kuantan. Meanwhile, the Pekan Campus is a modern waterfront campus dedicated primarily to computing,engineering and automotive technology, situated in Pahang's royal town. Together, both campuses provide state-of-the-art facilities and a dynamic learning environment for students.
